# 8 Forms of Entrepreneurship — Stories & Kahoot

**Presentation flow:** Teaser → Story → Reveal → 2-sentence explanation. Pause after each teaser so students can guess the company. Run a quick quiz after every 2–3 forms.

## Round 1 — Starting, partnering & innovating

### 1. Individual — eBay (Pierre Omidyar)
- **Teaser:** “Who would pay $15 for something that doesn't even work?”
- **Story:** In 1995, Pierre Omidyar built an auction website and sold a **broken laser pointer for $14.83**. The unlikely buyer helped prove that strangers would trade almost anything online.
- **Reveal:** **Pierre Omidyar — eBay | Individual entrepreneur.** An individual entrepreneur starts a business independently and makes their own decisions. They have autonomy but carry major responsibility.

### 2. Partner — Airbnb (Brian Chesky, Joe Gebbia & Nathan Blecharczyk)
- **Teaser:** “How did breakfast cereal help save a travel startup?”
- **Story:** In 2008, Airbnb was short on money. Co-founders Brian Chesky and Joe Gebbia designed election-themed cereal boxes—**Obama O's** and **Cap'n McCain's**—and sold them to help fund the business.
- **Reveal:** **Airbnb | Partner entrepreneur.** Partners launch a venture together and combine their skills. They share responsibilities, decisions and potential profits.

### 3. Innovative — Dyson (James Dyson)
- **Teaser:** “Would you keep trying after failing 5,126 times?”
- **Story:** James Dyson wanted a vacuum that wouldn't lose suction. He worked through **5,127 prototypes** before arriving at the design that launched his business.
- **Reveal:** **James Dyson — Dyson | Innovative entrepreneur.** Innovative entrepreneurs develop new products, methods or solutions. They use experimentation to solve problems in a different way.

### Kahoot Break #1
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Working alone = ? | **Individual** |
| Partners share responsibilities. True or false? | **True** |
| Dyson represents which form? | **Innovative** |

## Round 2 — Impact & rapid growth

### 4. Social — Empowerment Plan (Veronika Scott)
- **Teaser:** “What if a coat could become a sleeping bag—but the real solution was a job?”
- **Story:** Design student Veronika Scott created a coat that converts into a sleeping bag for people experiencing homelessness. After hearing that **jobs were needed as much as coats**, she founded Empowerment Plan, which employs people affected by homelessness to make them.
- **Reveal:** **Veronika Scott — Empowerment Plan | Social entrepreneur.** Social entrepreneurs aim to solve a social or environmental problem. They pair meaningful impact with a sustainable operating model.

### 5. High-growth — Shopify (Tobi Lütke and co-founders)
- **Teaser:** “How did an online snowboard shop become a giant e-commerce platform?”
- **Story:** The founders tried to sell snowboards online, but existing store-building software didn't meet their needs. They built their own tools and discovered that **other sellers needed them too**—leading to Shopify.
- **Reveal:** **Tobi Lütke — Shopify | High-growth entrepreneur.** High-growth entrepreneurs create businesses designed to scale quickly. They target large markets with models that can serve many customers.

### Kahoot Break #2
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Helping society = which form? | **Social** |
| Social entrepreneurs never earn money. True or false? | **False** |
| Which form aims to scale rapidly? | **High-growth** |

## Round 3 — Inside companies, families & across borders

### 6. Intrapreneur — Post-it Notes (Art Fry & Spencer Silver at 3M)
- **Teaser:** “How did ‘failed’ glue become an office essential?”
- **Story:** At 3M, Spencer Silver developed a removable adhesive that initially lacked an obvious use. Fellow employee Art Fry needed bookmarks that stayed in his hymnbook; together, their work led to **Post-it Notes**.
- **Reveal:** **Art Fry & Spencer Silver — 3M | Intrapreneurs.** Intrapreneurs develop new ideas while working inside an existing organization. They innovate using the company's resources instead of founding a separate business.

### 7. Family — LEGO (Kristiansen family)
- **Teaser:** “What if you accidentally burned down your father's workshop—and later led the family business?”
- **Story:** As a young child, Godtfred Kirk Christiansen was involved in an accident that caused a fire in his father's workshop. Ole Kirk Kristiansen rebuilt his business, later began making toys, and **Godtfred eventually led LEGO**.
- **Reveal:** **Kristiansen family — LEGO | Family entrepreneur.** Family entrepreneurs own or manage a business with relatives. Their businesses may be passed down through generations with a long-term vision.

### 8. International — AirAsia (Tony Fernandes and partners)
- **Teaser:** “Would you buy an airline for just 25 cents if it came with millions in debt?”
- **Story:** In 2001, Tony Fernandes and his partners acquired struggling AirAsia for a **symbolic 1 Malaysian ringgit**, taking on substantial debt and a tiny fleet. They turned it into a low-cost airline operating across national borders.
- **Reveal:** **Tony Fernandes — AirAsia | International entrepreneur.** International entrepreneurs enter and serve markets in more than one country. They expand their businesses beyond their home borders.

### Kahoot Break #3
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Innovating inside a company = ? | **Intrapreneur** |
| Family businesses can pass between generations. True or false? | **True** |
| Selling in several countries = ? | **International** |

## Final Kahoot Challenge — 60 Seconds

**Question for students to type:** **“Can you name all 8 forms of entrepreneurship we studied today?”**

**Answer key:** Individual · Partner · Innovative · Social · High-growth · Intrapreneur · Family · International

**Scoring:** 1 point per correct form (maximum 8).

**Closing line:** “Eight different forms, eight incredible stories—but they all began when someone saw an opportunity and acted on it.”

*Note: These examples are selected to illustrate one main form each; in real life, the forms can overlap.*
