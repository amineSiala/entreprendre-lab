## 3. The Story

In **2014**, Karim Beguir and Zohra Slim started a technology company in **Tunis** with **two laptops, $2,000, and a big idea**: use artificial intelligence to solve complex real-world problems.

Their company, **InstaDeep**, developed advanced AI systems used in fields such as logistics, transportation, and biotechnology. As it grew, InstaDeep worked with major international organizations and established an AI partnership with German biotechnology company **BioNTech**.

In **2023**, BioNTech completed its acquisition of InstaDeep. When the deal was announced, its potential value was reported at **up to £562 million (around $680 million)**, including conditional future payments.

**The reveal: InstaDeep — a startup born in Tunisia that became a global AI success story.**

## 4. Which Form of Entrepreneurship?

### Primary type: Innovative Entrepreneurship

**Innovative entrepreneurship** means creating new technologies, products, or methods to solve problems differently. InstaDeep fits because its business was built on sophisticated AI research and applications.

### Other forms that apply

| Form | Why InstaDeep fits |
|---|---|
| **Partner entrepreneurship** | It was founded by two entrepreneurs: Karim Beguir and Zohra Slim. |
| **High-growth entrepreneurship** | It grew rapidly from a small Tunisian company into a major international AI business. |
| **International entrepreneurship** | It established operations and partnerships in multiple countries. |

> **Teaching point:** One business can represent several forms of entrepreneurship at the same time.
